# Beware of These Warning Signs

+   - Unsolicited messages that urge you to respond quickly
+   - Deals and offers that seem too good to be true
+   - Texts from friends or strangers that ask for money or wire transfers
+   - Shortened URLs in messages that tempt you to click

# Keep in Mind...

+   - It's easy to impersonate trusted people and companies in texts
+   - Your smartphone is a gateway to sensitive business and personal data
+   - Claims and offers should be verified via known websites or phone numbers
